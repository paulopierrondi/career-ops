# Kommo AI Salesbot Clinic/Courses Checklist

Purpose: prepare a safe first phase for clinic, aesthetic, course or service buyers who need Kommo CRM setup plus WhatsApp/Instagram intake, AI qualification and sales follow-up.

Do not store real contacts, WhatsApp numbers, Instagram messages, payment data, credentials, cookies, tokens or clinic/customer PII in this file or in reports.

## First Phase Scope

- Audit the current funnel, channels and contact base before importing or automating anything.
- Configure Kommo users, groups, permissions, pipelines, custom fields and loss reasons for one primary funnel.
- Clean and import only the minimum approved contact fields from the client's spreadsheet.
- Connect one channel first: official WhatsApp/API path or approved Kommo-compatible connector; add Instagram only after the first path works.
- Build one Salesbot or webhook-backed AI qualification path for inbound leads, with JSON-style field extraction and human review.
- Add one high-intent handoff rule: course interest, clinic appointment intent, price objection, financing question or callback request.
- Deliver a short admin runbook, test script, field map and phase-2 backlog.

## Intake Questions

1. Is Kommo already active, or is this a fresh workspace?
2. Which funnel matters first: aesthetic clinic appointments, course sales, post-lead nurturing or reactivation?
3. What is the source and consent basis for the existing contact base?
4. Which WhatsApp and Instagram accounts are already approved for business use?
5. Should AI answer only FAQs, qualify leads, recommend next steps or draft human replies?
6. Which fields must the AI never infer or overwrite without human approval?
7. Which calendar, payment, course platform or ad source must be connected in phase 2?

## Delivery Checks

- Use a redacted sample spreadsheet before importing the full contact base.
- Deduplicate contacts by client-approved rules before any bulk import.
- Keep AI writes limited to explicit CRM fields such as need, source, urgency, budget range, preferred contact window and next action.
- Require human approval before bulk messages, reactivation campaigns, deletions, merges or pipeline-wide updates.
- Validate WhatsApp opt-in, Instagram messaging rules, LGPD basis and unsubscribe/escalation language.
- Log channel event, CRM lead id, changed field, bot confidence and human override path.
- Keep payment, health, medical or aesthetic claims under human review.

## Proposal Angle

Lead with a first working revenue path, not a full CRM transformation: "I will configure the Kommo foundation, import the approved base safely and make one WhatsApp/Instagram AI qualification flow work end to end with human review. After that, we can expand to campaigns, agenda, dashboards and more funnels."
