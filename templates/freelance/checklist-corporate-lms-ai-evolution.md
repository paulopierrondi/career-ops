# Corporate LMS AI Evolution Checklist

Use for LMS/EAD evolution opportunities where the buyer requires real LMS maintenance or development experience plus AI applied to learning flows.

## Fit Gate

- Confirm Paulo has verifiable LMS/EAD case proof before bidding as implementer.
- If proof is weak, propose only a diagnostic, architecture review or AI feature plan.
- Avoid claiming SCORM, xAPI, LTI or platform-specific LMS production experience unless verified.

## Intake

- Identify current LMS stack, hosting, auth, roles, course model and content formats.
- Confirm active-user volume, admin workflows, reporting needs and current pain points.
- List AI use cases separately: search, tutor, quiz generation, summary, recommendation, support triage or analytics.
- Confirm accessibility, audit, data retention and learner privacy expectations.

## Data Boundary

- No student exports, credentials, cookies, production database dumps or private course files in chat/repo/email.
- Use redacted examples and client-controlled access after contract.
- Keep AI outputs advisory and instructor/admin-reviewed for assessments or learning progression decisions.

## First Phase

- Map the LMS architecture and user journeys.
- Select one low-risk AI feature with measurable value, such as admin content search or lesson summary draft.
- Define required data sources, embedding/search boundaries and reviewer workflow.
- Deliver a rollout plan, acceptance tests and backlog instead of full LMS rebuild.

## QA

- Test role permissions, course visibility, content freshness, hallucination controls and accessibility basics.
- Validate logs, fallback behavior and admin override.
- Run mobile/responsive checks for learner-facing changes.

## Proposal Angle

Position as a controlled AI evolution plan for an existing LMS. If LMS proof is not strong, lead with diagnostic and AI architecture, not hands-on platform ownership.
